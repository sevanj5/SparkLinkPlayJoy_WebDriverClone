Combo sequence editor dry run

Builds the exact macro frames for a sequence of key combos and shows them. This page has no device access: nothing here is sent to the keyboard.

Sequence

One combo per line: combo [waitMs]. Modifiers: ctrl, shift, alt, win. Example: ctrl+shift+c 100. Lines starting with # are ignored.

Your keyboard's slots 0–2 already hold macros (seen in the read-only probe); a spare slot such as 3 is the safe choice.
